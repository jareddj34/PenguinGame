# Gemini Prompt: Penguin Game — Single-Zone Map

Copy everything below the line into Gemini to generate a top-down game map.

---

**Generate a top-down (bird's-eye) game design map for a single zone of a 3D action-adventure game.**

## Style & Reference
The game is inspired by *The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening* (the 2019 remake's soft, toy-like, slightly tilt-shifted look). Render the map as a clean, top-down level layout — readable like a game designer's blueprint, but visually appealing with charming, cozy art direction. Use a warm, inviting palette dominated by cool snow-whites, icy blues, and soft shadows, with pops of color from objects and NPCs. The player character is a small, round penguin.

## Setting
This is a **single explorable zone**, majority snow and ice, with a few mixed-biome pockets for visual variety. The zone should feel like one cohesive arctic region you could walk across in a few minutes.

Lay the zone out with these connected sub-areas:
- **Igloo Village (center-ish):** A small cluster of 3–4 igloos with round entrances. This is the social hub where NPC penguins stand around to talk to. Include a signpost and some packed-snow paths connecting the igloos.
- **Frozen Lake (one side):** A wide sheet of smooth ice — visually distinct, glossy, with cracks. Good for the player to slide across on their belly. Add a few breakable ice blocks scattered on and around it.
- **Snowfield / Open Plains:** Rolling open snow with scattered snow mounds, small drifts, and clusters of throwable snowballs the player can pick up. This is the main combat space where enemy seals roam.
- **Ice Cave or Cliff edge:** A rockier, darker pocket — exposed grey stone and ice formations — that contrasts the white snow. Hide a treasure chest here.
- **Mixed-biome fringe:** Along one border, transition into a small contrasting biome — a stand of snow-dusted evergreen pines, or a rocky/pebbled shoreline meeting dark water with floating chunks of ice. Keep it a minority of the map for variety.

## Objects & Mechanics to Show on the Map
Place and clearly mark these interactive elements so I can replicate them in Unity:
- **Enemies — Seals:** 2–4 hostile seals patrolling the snowfield and near the lake.
- **Sword & Shield pickups:** A starting sword and a shield placed as collectible items (e.g., one near the village, one guarded).
- **Snowballs:** Small piles of throwable snowballs in the open snowfield.
- **Breakable ice blocks:** Cube-shaped ice blocks that can be smashed, some blocking paths or hiding items.
- **Igloo entrances:** Doorways that lead to separate interior scenes (mark them as "→ interior").
- **Treasure chests:** 2–3 closed chests, at least one tucked behind an obstacle or in the cave.
- **NPCs:** A few friendly penguin characters in the village to talk to.
- **Paths & gating:** Show the natural walking routes, plus at least one spot where a breakable ice block or enemy gates access to a reward.

## Layout Requirements
- Top-down / overhead perspective, the whole zone visible in one frame.
- Clear, legible composition — distinct sub-areas with natural borders (cliffs, water, tree lines, ice edges) separating them.
- Label or visually distinguish each interactive object (sword, shield, snowballs, ice blocks, chests, igloos, seals, NPCs).
- Include a small legend/key in a corner identifying the icons.
- Leave the layout readable as a design reference, not cluttered.

## Mood
Cozy, adventurous, family-friendly. A peaceful arctic world with gentle danger — the kind of place a brave little penguin sets out to explore.
